Ace of Angels

A Living Game

The online 3D space combat sim by Flying Rock Enterprises, winner of Technical Excellence at the 2002 Independent Games Festival. Its servers went dark; this project brings it back.

This is Ace of Angels, the PC space combat game from 2002, not the K-pop group AOA ("Ace of Angels").

The game

Ace of Angels was an online space fighter game built by a small team in Marietta, Georgia, "since 1996". Set in the early 26th century in the Known Sphere (8 races, 10 nations), it promised 40+ fighters, capital ships and persistent faction politics. It won Technical Excellence at the 2002 Independent Games Festival, shipped free betas on magazine cover discs from 2002 to 2004, and was scheduled for release in March 2006. It never launched. Its servers went dark, and the company was dissolved in 2011.

What the project has done

Six builds recovered

Client versions 2.0.0 to 2.9.2, found on magazine cover discs and in public archives, each downloaded, hashed and versioned.

Runs on Windows 11

Crash, timing and display fixes applied to your own copy, all reversible. The offline training mission plays. Windows 10 has not been tried yet.

Multiplayer again

A new stand-in server, written from reverse engineering, lets friends dogfight over a LAN, with a host window and a Linux service.

No game files are stored in the project: the tools fetch the original installer from public archives into your own folder and change only your copy. What is verified and what is still untested is listed on the status page.

Play it

  1. Install Python 3.10 or later, with Add python.exe to PATH ticked.
  2. Download the project from GitHub (Code → Download ZIP) and extract it.
  3. Double-click Play.cmd and follow its setup checklist. It downloads the 2.9.2 installer from archive.org after asking, applies the fixes and helps you add the graphics wrapper.

To host for friends, double-click Host.cmd, choose who can join and press Start. Full guide: playing, hosting.
